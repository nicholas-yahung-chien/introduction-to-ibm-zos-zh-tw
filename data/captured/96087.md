### Diving deeper into z/OS

IBM releases a new version of z/OS every two years. Updating the z/OS on a system isn't trivial, and usually takes months of planning. Each z/OS brings new features to the Mainframe server, and just like with other devices, some of these features are only available on the latest hardware.

I​t's a good time to review some of the technical documents that come with a new release of z/OS. You won't be tested on this, and please don't read too heavily into these documents, but they'll make for great references later on in your career.
